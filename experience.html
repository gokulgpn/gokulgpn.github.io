Bewertung der Erreichbarkeit von Einzelhandelsgeschäften mit bestimmten Verkehrsmitteln in ausgewählten deutschen Städten
Evaluation of Mode-Specific Retail Accessibility in Selected German Cities

Evaluated retail accessibility across five western-German cities using r5py (R5 routing engine) for walking, cycling, public transport and car. Accessibility was modelled on a 100 m Zensus 2022 grid and related to seven socioeconomic indicators. Spatial patterns and inequalities were assessed through bivariate mapping and Spearman correlation, with results visualised in QGIS.
