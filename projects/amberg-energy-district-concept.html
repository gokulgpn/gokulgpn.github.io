Overview

An energy district concept for the City of Amberg in Bavaria, built around a heat atlas for the Luitpoldhöhe redevelopment area. The project set out to improve energy efficiency across the district and unlock new potential for heat supply, starting from a detailed survey of renovation status and building age classes.

From that baseline, a comprehensive catalogue of measures for energy retrofitting was developed — including the option of a district heating network — with each measure evaluated on cost-effectiveness, security of supply, social acceptability and ecological efficiency. Close cooperation with the municipal utilities and participation by local residents were central to the concept's acceptance.

I carried out the building-level heat demand and building-stock analysis and produced the cartography and WebGIS for the project, working alongside colleagues at Plan4Better.

Client: Stadt Amberg · Period: 2024

Outcome

Delivered a heat atlas and energy district concept that gives the City of Amberg a building-level picture of heat demand in the Luitpoldhöhe redevelopment area, a prioritised catalogue of retrofitting measures assessed against economic, social and ecological criteria, and a WebGIS through which the municipal utilities and the public can explore the results.
