Overview

A municipal heating plan for the City of Mainburg (around 15,000 residents) in Bavaria, developed by Plan4Better in collaboration with a partner organisation. The goal is a comprehensive heating plan that enables a greenhouse-gas-neutral heat supply by 2045.

A detailed status analysis integrated heat density maps, energy carrier shares, building types, renovation status and GIS data on gas and district heating networks, with a digital twin serving as the central data foundation. The potential analysis explored energy savings, renewable energy and waste-heat use, and the suitability of areas for district heating networks versus individual heating solutions. A target scenario for 2045 set out clear measures for reducing greenhouse gas emissions, improving resource efficiency and integrating renewable sources, backed by a measure catalogue with cost, funding and climate-protection profiles for each option.

I carried out the status and potential analysis and produced the cartography and WebGIS for the project, working alongside the partner organisation and colleagues at Plan4Better.

Client: Stadt Mainburg · Period: 2024–2025
